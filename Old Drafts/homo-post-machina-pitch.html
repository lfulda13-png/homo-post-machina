11 · Cost
Implementation

Pilot first, then scale

Year 1Pilot
  • DG EMPL taskforce with the AI Office, DLA as co-designer
  • Campaign launches
  • Database built on worker wage data
Years 2–3Rollout
  • Common clauses in EU institutions’ AI tenders
  • Vendors disclose subcontractors and pay
  • Fair AI label starts as voluntary scheme
Year 4Stage gate
  • Independent evaluation
  • Wages, compliance costs, market effects, relocation of work
Year 5+EU market
  • Minimum requirements for all AI products
  • Commission proposal, approved by Parliament and Council
Self-correcting: anonymous worker reports and annual audits update the ratings · label thresholds tighten every two years
